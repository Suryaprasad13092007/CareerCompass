# Career Compass - Project Vision

## Problem Statement
Many engineering students are confused about career paths, required skills, and placement preparation.

Career Compass helps students identify suitable career paths, track their progress, and generate learning roadmaps.

## Target Users
- 1st Year Students
- 2nd Year Students
- 3rd Year Students
- Placement Aspirants

## Main Features
- Student Profile Management
- Career Recommendation
- Roadmap Generation
- Skill Tracking
- Career Readiness Score
